# Living Principles

These principles are the decision compass for Mycelium AI. They are derived from the [origin story](origin_story.md), not a marketing layer added afterwards. A contribution that conflicts with them needs an explicit discussion.

1. **Waste is a resource in the wrong place.** Prefer repair, reuse and modest requirements over premature replacement.
2. **Hardware deserves a second life.** A useful feature must remain usable on old laptops, mini PCs and low-power devices whenever practical.
3. **Technology should increase autonomy.** A local community must retain meaningful control over its data, tools and ability to repair them.
4. **Knowledge should be open and shareable where responsible.** Keep provenance, consent, privacy and licences intact.
5. **Energy is a real constraint.** Make consumption visible; defer non-urgent work when power is scarce and always allow a person to override automation.
6. **Local-first is a capability.** The system should continue to serve a local purpose when the internet or a cloud provider disappears.
7. **Technology supports life.** Measure success by the benefit to people, animals and ecosystems—not merely throughput, novelty or growth.

## A practical decision check

Before merging a meaningful feature, ask:

- Does it solve a real local problem more simply than the alternative?
- Can someone in a small community install, understand and repair it?
- Does it introduce a new dependency, data exposure or energy cost?
- Is there a safe offline and low-resource path?

The answer does not need to be perfect. It does need to be visible.
